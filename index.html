The House Keeps You

HOW TO SURVIVE — scrawled inside the front cover of Edmund's notebook

  • Walk, don't run. Running and creaky floorboards (the faint dark planks) carry a long way. Crouching (C) is almost silent.
  • Watch the beeper. Once it reaches "Getting closer", find a hiding spot.
  • Turn the torch off (F) when you hide, or they'll see the light.
  • Don't let them see you hide, or they'll drag you out. Break line of sight first.
  • Wait after the beeping stops before coming out. Sometimes the Mimic silences the beeper while it's still nearby.
  • Don't hide under beds in the basement — the Crawling Thing checks under them. Wardrobes and cabinets are safer.
  • Grab spare batteries, from drawers and loose on the floors.
  • Ignore voices calling for help. That's the Mimic.
  • If you forget a clue, press J to re-read every note you've found.
  • Follow the clue sensor. Its soft ping gets faster — and the meter hotter — as you near your next goal. Press N for directions.
  • Throw bottles (G or right-click) to lure monsters away. If one loses sight of you, the smash pulls it off your trail.

Your progress saves automatically. Press H to read these tips again; brightness, sensitivity and hints are in Settings (P to pause).
